Built for real-time parking

Core capabilities exposed by the Dwara backend.

QR Authentication

Short-lived QR credentials authenticate vehicles at connected entry and exit gates.

Per-Second Billing

Parking fees are calculated from the exact duration between entry and exit timestamps.

IoT Gate Control

Cloud-connected ESP32 gate controllers communicate with the backend to operate physical barriers.

Cloud Backend

Parking sessions, facilities, vehicles and payments are managed through a centralized backend.

Digital Payments

Completed parking sessions generate payment transactions before the exit gate is released.

Controller Monitoring

Gate controllers periodically report their health through heartbeat signals to maintain infrastructure visibility.

System architecture

Dwara connects the driver's application to physical parking infrastructure.
